For Marantz owners on iPhone and iPad

The Marantz receiver app for iPhone

Looking for a Marantz app for iPhone? AVR Maestro is an independent remote app for network-enabled Marantz AV receivers, on iPhone and iPad from the App Store. It controls the receiver directly over your home network: power, volume, inputs, surround modes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, multi-zone and one-tap Scenes. One-time purchase, no ads, no subscriptions, no cloud account.

Built for iPhone

What your Marantz gets on iPhone

Everything the app does on Android, plus the parts that make an iPhone a better Marantz remote than the one in the box.

📱

Home screen widgets

Quick Control (power, volume, mute, source), Now Playing and Status widgets on your iPhone. When the receiver is off, the Quick Control power button stays live so you can wake it from the widget.

🔊

Your volume buttons

The iPhone's own volume buttons drive the Marantz. In the app, hold any volume button to ramp.

🎭

One-tap Scenes

Save input, volume, sound mode, Audyssey or Dirac, speaker preset, zones and the tuner, and recall all of it with a single tap.

🎲

Audyssey & Dirac Live

Switch MultEQ curves, Dynamic EQ and Dynamic Volume, or pick Dirac Live filter slots by name, on receivers that expose them.

⭐

Smart Select, named right

Your receiver's own memory slots, named the way your Marantz names them: Smart Select. Tap to recall, long-press to rename or save.

💬

The on-device assistant

Ask your receiver in plain words on an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26). It runs on the device, not in the cloud.

Common questions

Marantz on iPhone, answered

Is there a Marantz app for iPhone besides the official one?
Yes. AVR Maestro is an independent app for Marantz receivers on iPhone and iPad, from the App Store. It connects over your network using the receiver's native protocols and adds one-tap Scenes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, speaker presets and multi-zone. See why owners switch.
Does it work with my Marantz receiver?
It works with network-enabled Marantz AV receivers from roughly 2014 on, on the same Wi-Fi or LAN as your iPhone. No cloud account or extra hardware. See the supported receivers.
Does it work on iPad and Mac too?
Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad from the App Store, and on Mac from the Mac App Store. There are versions for Android and Windows as well.
Is it free? Any ads or subscriptions?
It is a one-time purchase with no ads, no subscriptions and no account, and it controls your receiver directly over your local network.
