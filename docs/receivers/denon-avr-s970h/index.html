Common questions

Denon AVR-S970H + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Denon AVR-S970H?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller for the S970H. Over your network it controls surround modes, Audyssey MultEQ, speaker presets, levels, multi-zone, the tuner and one-tap Scenes.
Does the S970H support Dirac Live in the app?
The Denon AVR-S970H uses Audyssey MultEQ and does not support Dirac Live - that is a hardware limit of this model, not the app. AVR Maestro controls Audyssey MultEQ, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume and LFC on the S970H, live from your phone. See the Audyssey & Dirac guide.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the S970H directly over your local network. No cloud account, no subscription, no extra hardware, and your receiver's configuration never leaves your network.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the S970H, grab its IP, and add it in the app. Full steps on the getting started guide.
