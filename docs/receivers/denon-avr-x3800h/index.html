Common questions

Denon AVR-X3800H + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Denon AVR-X3800H?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller for the X3800H. Over your network it controls surround modes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, speaker presets, levels, multi-zone, the tuner and one-tap Scenes.
Does it control Dirac Live on the X3800H?
Yes. With a Dirac Live license it switches your filter slots by name; on a standard Audyssey preset it controls MultEQ, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume and LFC. See the Audyssey & Dirac guide.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the X3800H directly over your local network. No cloud account, no subscription and no extra hardware.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the X3800H, grab its IP, and add it in the app. Full steps on the getting started guide.
