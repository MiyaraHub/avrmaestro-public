Common questions

Marantz AV10 + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Marantz AV10?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller for the AV10. Over your network it controls surround modes, Audyssey MultEQ XT32 and Dirac Live, speaker presets, levels, multi-zone, the tuner and one-tap Scenes.
Does AVR Maestro control Dirac Live on the AV10?
Yes. On a Marantz AV10 with a Dirac Live license, AVR Maestro lists your Dirac filter slots by name and switches the active one. With a standard Audyssey preset it controls MultEQ, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume and LFC instead. See the Audyssey & Dirac guide.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the AV10 directly over your local network. No cloud account, no subscription, no extra hardware, and your receiver's configuration never leaves your network.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the AV10, grab its IP, and add it in the app. Full steps on the getting started guide.
