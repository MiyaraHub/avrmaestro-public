Not sure?

The real test isn't the model - it's the network

AVR Maestro reaches your receiver over your local network, so compatibility comes down to whether the receiver is reachable, not just its model number.

  • The receiver's own web portal loads in a browser on your phone.
  • The HEOS app finds and controls the receiver.
  • The official Denon or Marantz app connects to it.
  • Its network ports aren't blocked by a guest network, IoT VLAN or strict firewall.
If those can all talk to your receiver, AVR Maestro will see it too - and it reads your specific model's capabilities on connect, only showing controls your chassis actually exposes.

A note on older firmware

On some pre-2025 receivers (the X4500H, X6700H, S960H and X4700H class), the receiver can't serve the Speaker Layout or Subwoofer Output screens to the app over the network. AVR Maestro hands you a one-tap button into the receiver's own web control for those edits - the rest of the app works normally. Receivers on newer firmware serve every screen directly.

Common questions

Receiver support, answered

Is my Denon or Marantz receiver supported by AVR Maestro?
AVR Maestro works with network-enabled Denon and Marantz AV receivers from roughly 2014 onward - including the Marantz Cinema 30, 40, 50 and 60, Denon AVR-A1H, AVR-X1800H through X6800H, AVC-X8500HA, AVR-S770H and S970H, and the Marantz SR and AV series. If your receiver's web portal, the HEOS app and the official Denon or Marantz app can all reach it on your network, AVR Maestro can too.
Does AVR Maestro work with the Marantz Cinema 30?
Yes. The Marantz Cinema 30 is fully supported, including its surround modes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, speaker presets, multi-zone and tuner - all over your local network.
Does AVR Maestro work with the Denon AVR-X3800H or AVR-A1H?
Yes. Both the Denon AVR-X3800H and the flagship AVR-A1H are supported, with full access to sound modes, room correction, speaker setup, channel levels and multi-zone control.
