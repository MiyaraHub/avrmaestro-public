Denon AVR-X4000 remote app: control it from your phone

The AVR-X4000 is a Denon receiver from the older generation. AVR Maestro controls it from your phone using the commands that receiver understands, and hides the controls it cannot answer. No cloud account, no subscriptions, no ads.

Everything the AVR-X4000 does

What works on the Denon AVR-X4000

On the Denon AVR-X4000, AVR Maestro controls power, inputs, the surround modes the receiver reports, Audyssey MultEQ, tone and channel levels. Browsing internet radio and media server menus and the speaker layout are not available yet.

Surround modes

The surround modes your receiver reports.

Audyssey & tone

Switch Audyssey MultEQ modes and set tone, bass, treble and channel levels.

Now playing

Title and station show for internet radio. Browsing its menus below the top level is not available yet.

Speaker setup

Layout, distances and crossovers are not available on this receiver yet. Channel levels are on the Sound tab.

Remote keys

Information, Setup, Option and Back do not work on this receiver yet. Power, inputs and System do.

Built from the command list

Zone 2, Zone 3, favorites, the tuner, HD Radio and Scenes follow Denon's command list and are not yet confirmed on a real AVR-X4000.

The AVR-X4000 predates the modern Denon protocol, so AVR Maestro reads what it reports and offers only those controls. Bluetooth, Loudness and Cinema EQ are not shown because that chassis cannot answer them. It has no multi-room speaker controls.
Common questions

Denon AVR-X4000 + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Denon AVR-X4000?
Yes, with limits. AVR Maestro controls the AVR-X4000 over your network: power, inputs, surround modes, Audyssey, tone and channel levels work. Browsing internet radio menus and the speaker layout are not available yet.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the AVR-X4000 directly over your local network. No cloud account, no subscription and no extra hardware.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the AVR-X4000, then open Add Receiver and tap it in the list (or enter its IP address if nothing answers). Full steps on the getting started guide.
