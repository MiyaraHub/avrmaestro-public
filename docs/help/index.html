Help & troubleshooting

Fix common Denon & Marantz app problems

Quick fixes for the most common questions. If you're still stuck, send us your logs from Settings › About AVR Maestro › All Relevant Logs, the fastest way to get support - reach the developer in the Discord or the Facebook support group, replies are quick.

Connection

Getting connected

"Test Connection" fails
Make sure the phone and receiver are on the same Wi-Fi network and subnet - mesh systems, guest networks and IoT VLANs can isolate devices. On the receiver, Setup → Network → Network Control must be On. Re-check the IP against the receiver's own Network → Information screen, since DHCP addresses change. HTTP is required; Telnet and HEOS are nice-to-have. If the receiver is reachable but its network stack is saturated - too many controller apps holding connections - the app says so with a clear network busy message instead of a generic failure; close another controller or give it a few seconds. Full steps on the getting started guide.
Connection drops every few seconds
Receiver Telnet ports can get sticky after long uptimes - power-cycle the receiver from the wall to clear the listener. On Wi-Fi 6 mesh, force the phone onto the same band as the receiver (AVRs usually sit on 2.4 GHz; phones prefer 5 GHz).
How do I know my receiver is supported?
It comes down to your network, not just the model. If the receiver's web portal, the HEOS app and the official Denon/Marantz app can all reach it, AVR Maestro should too. See the supported receivers page.
Controls & display

Why something looks off

The volume readout looks wrong
The volume can be shown on two scales: the 0-98 scale, or the decibel scale from -80 to +18 dB. Both describe the same volume. In Settings › Manage Receivers › your receiver › Audio, Show absolute volume on top chooses which one is the big number, and Remote volume display chooses the scale used on the Remote tab. See the Volume guide.
Sound modes or feature pills are greyed out / "N/A"
Surround modes and features are gated by the input's audio format and the active sound mode - a 2-channel source won't enable Dolby Atmos. That's the receiver's behavior, not the app's. The Locked Controls guide explains why a control is locked and what unlocks it.
Some Sound or Speaker tabs are missing
Tabs are model- and mode-aware. Audyssey, Dirac Live, IMAX, Auro-3D, Surround Shape, Distances and Crossovers only appear on receivers that expose them. Check Settings › Manage Receivers › your receiver › Device Information to see what the app detected. A sub-tab you switched off comes back from Settings › Customize Tabs, and a bottom tab from Settings › Customize Navigation. See the Navigation guide.
The Radio tab isn't showing
Radio is capability-gated: it only appears on receivers with a built-in FM/AM tuner. If yours has one but the tab is hidden, confirm the tuner appears as an input on the receiver and re-enable it from Settings › Customize Navigation. See the Radio guide.
Now Playing card is blank
The current input doesn't expose metadata - analog, optical/coaxial and most HDMI passthrough sources don't report track data. For HEOS sources, tap Launch HEOS to start playback, then return and the metadata populates.
The home-screen widget says "AVR off" or "Standby"
That's the widget's off-state, not a fault. The Quick Control and Status widgets (iOS and Android) show "Standby" when the receiver is in standby and "AVR off" when they cannot reach it, instead of the last input and volume, so you never read stale data. On Android the Quick Control volume and mute buttons hide in that state, but the power button stays live - tap it to wake the receiver straight from the widget (Network Standby must be enabled on the receiver). See the Widgets guide.
Some speaker screens open in the receiver's web control
Some Denon and Marantz firmware does not return Speaker Layout, Speaker Distance or Speaker Crossover to the app. The page then says so, with Retry and a button that opens that screen in the receiver's own web control. If you never use it, hide the tab in Settings › Customize Tabs. If the receiver's web control page is open in a browser, close it first, since it can keep the receiver busy.
A Scene did not fully apply
A receiver can refuse a setting that the current input, signal or sound mode does not allow. The Scenes guide explains what to check in its section on when a Scene does not fully apply.
Support

Get help and send logs

Everything for support is in Settings › About AVR Maestro, under Support and Community.

  • Discord Community: join the MiyaraHub community and ask the developer directly.
  • Facebook Support Group: AVR Maestro users and Q&A.
  • All Relevant Logs: every app and receiver log in one zip file, ready to send us.
  1. Connect to the receiver you have a problem with, and if you can, make the problem happen once.
  2. Open Settings › About AVR Maestro and find All Relevant Logs.
  3. Tap Download to save the zip, Share to send it with any app, or Email to attach it to an email. While it works, the app shows what it is collecting and how far along it is.
  4. Send the zip to us on Discord or in the Facebook group with a short description of what went wrong.
The zip includes the app's Debug Log and what the app read from your receiver, such as its device information and capabilities. If your device has no email app, Email opens the share sheet instead.

Still stuck? We're quick to help

Send All Relevant Logs from Settings › About AVR Maestro and reach the developer on Discord or Facebook - replies are fast.
