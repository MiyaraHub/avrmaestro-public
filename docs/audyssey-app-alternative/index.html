An Audyssey app alternative for everyday control

Looking for an Audyssey app alternative? AVR Maestro switches your Denon or Marantz receiver's Audyssey settings from your phone: the MultEQ curve (Reference, Bypass L+R, Flat or Off), Dynamic EQ, Reference Level Offset, Dynamic Volume and LFC. It does not run the calibration: the mic and the Audyssey app still do that, and custom target curves need the paid MultEQ Editor app.

What AVR Maestro does, and what it does not

AVR Maestro is not a calibration tool. It puts the day-to-day control in your pocket and leaves the measuring to the tools built for it.

Common questions

Audyssey from your phone, answered

Is there an alternative to the Audyssey app for daily use?
Yes. AVR Maestro reads and switches MultEQ (Reference / Bypass L+R / Flat / Off), Dynamic EQ, Reference Level Offset, Dynamic Volume and LFC on Denon and Marantz receivers that have Audyssey, right from the app.
Can AVR Maestro calibrate my room?
No. AVR Maestro does not run the calibration. The mic and the Audyssey or Dirac Live software do that; the app controls the result day to day.
Can I edit Audyssey target curves in AVR Maestro?
No. Custom Audyssey target curves need the paid MultEQ Editor app. AVR Maestro does have a 9-band Graphic EQ that can start from your measured Audyssey curve, usable when MultEQ and Dirac are off.
Does it work with Dirac Live too?
Yes. With a Dirac Live license on the receiver, the app lists your filter slots by name and switches the active one in a tap.
