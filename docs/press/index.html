AVR Maestro press kit

Everything you need to write about AVR Maestro: what it is, the facts that do not change, the app icon and screenshots you may use.

What it is

AVR Maestro is an independent network remote for Denon and Marantz AV receivers. It talks to the receiver over your local network, with no account, no ads and no cloud service in between, and it shows only what your receiver model actually has.

Key facts

  • Price: $9.99, paid once. No subscription and no ads. The optional Home Automation Pro add-on is $2.99.
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad, Mac, Android, and Windows.
  • Receivers: Denon and Marantz network receivers; see the receiver list.
  • Languages: 30.
  • Maker: MiyaraHub Technologies LLC.
  • Privacy: Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics go to Firebase to help fix bugs. See the privacy policy.
