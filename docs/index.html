Scenes

Your whole room, one tap.

Movie night means the right input, the right sound mode, Dirac or Audyssey the way you like it, the center lifted a touch and Zone 2 off. A Scene does all of that at once. AVR Maestro builds it with you, step by step, so you never have to remember which menu hides what.

  1. Say what it is for. Movies and TV, Music, Gaming, Late night, Other rooms or Custom, and the builder points out the settings that usually fit.
  2. Choose what it changes. Input, volume, sound mode, Audyssey and Dirac, speaker levels, zones and more, grouped the way you think about them. Nothing is ticked for you, and if your receiver cannot apply something right now, it tells you why the moment you tick it.
  3. Review and save. Name it, give it an icon and a color, and check every setting it will store. Saving changes nothing on your receiver; the Scene only acts when you tap it.
  4. Tap it anywhere. From the Scenes tab, the Home screen, an Android widget or a shortcut, or let Home Assistant run it while your phone stays in your pocket.

Common questions

Denon & Marantz control, answered

Is there a better app to control a Denon or Marantz receiver?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller built specifically for Denon and Marantz AV receivers. It surfaces controls the official apps bury, adds one-tap Scenes the official apps don't have, and reaches Audyssey, Dirac Live, speaker presets, multi-zone and the tuner from one place.
Does AVR Maestro work with my Denon or Marantz receiver?
It works with modern network-enabled Denon and Marantz AV receivers - the Cinema series (Cinema 30/40/50/60), the Denon AVR-X and AVR-A series (X1800H through AVR-A1H), the SR/NR series and more. AVR Maestro reads each receiver's capabilities on connect and only shows what yours actually exposes. See the full list of supported receivers.
Do I need a subscription or a cloud account?
No. AVR Maestro is a one-time purchase - no subscriptions, no ads, no account. It talks to your receiver directly over your local network, with no cloud service in between. Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics go to Firebase to help fix bugs and improve the app. Home Automation (Home Assistant, Hubitat, webhooks) is an optional one-time unlock inside the app.
Can it control Audyssey MultEQ and Dirac Live?
Yes. AVR Maestro switches Audyssey MultEQ curves, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume and LFC, and on a Dirac Live receiver it lists your filter slots by name and switches the active one. See the Audyssey & Dirac control guide.
Does it support multi-zone (Zone 2 and Zone 3)?
Yes - independent power, input, volume and Smart Select / Quick Select recall for Zone 2 and Zone 3, so you can run whole-house audio from your phone.
Is AVR Maestro made by Denon or Marantz?
No. AVR Maestro is an independent app made by MiyaraHub Technologies LLC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Denon, Marantz, Sound United or Masimo Corporation. It controls your receiver over your local network using the receiver's own network protocol.
