For Denon owners on Android

The Denon receiver app for Android

Looking for a Denon app for Android? AVR Maestro is an independent remote app for network-enabled Denon AV receivers, on Android phones and tablets from Google Play. It controls the receiver directly over your home network: power, volume, inputs, surround modes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, multi-zone and one-tap Scenes. One-time purchase, no ads, no subscriptions, no cloud account.

Built for Android

What your Denon gets on Android

Everything the app does, on the phone you already carry, with home screen widgets that work without opening the app.

Home screen widgets

Quick Control, Now Playing, Signal Status, Scenes and Multi-Room widgets on your home screen. While the receiver is off, the Quick Control widget keeps its power button so you can wake it from there.

Your volume buttons

Your phone's own volume buttons drive the Denon. In the app, hold any volume button to ramp.

One-tap Scenes

Save input, volume, sound mode, Audyssey or Dirac, speaker preset, zones and the tuner, and recall all of it with a single tap.

Audyssey & Dirac Live

Switch MultEQ curves, Dynamic EQ and Dynamic Volume, or pick Dirac Live filter slots by name, on receivers that expose them.

Quick Select, named right

Your receiver's own memory slots, named the way your Denon names them: Quick Select. Tap to recall, long-press to rename or save.

Home automation

Link Home Assistant, Hubitat or webhooks so your scenes run from your smart home while the phone stays in your pocket. An optional one-time unlock inside the app.

More in the app

Built for the receiver you own

Multi-Room

Control the speakers and groups of HEOS-enabled receivers from the Home tab: group them, browse a music service, search it for stations, tracks or artists, and manage the queue.

Search for any setting

Type a setting's name on the Home tab and jump straight to it. Search works in every language and follows the receiver you have selected.

HDMI Output card

Add an HDMI Output card to the Home tab: Auto, TV 1 or TV 2, with the output your receiver reports lit in your accent color.

Custom sleep timers

Off, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes, plus three custom slots of 1 to 120 minutes that you save once and reuse.

Older Denon receivers too

The Denon AVR-X4000 is supported, with controls the chassis cannot answer left out rather than shown dead.

Common questions

Denon on Android, answered

Is there a Denon app for Android besides the official one?
Yes. AVR Maestro is an independent app for Denon receivers on Android phones and tablets, from Google Play. It connects over your network using the receiver's native protocols and adds one-tap Scenes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, speaker presets and multi-zone. See why owners switch.
Does it work with my Denon receiver?
It works with network-enabled Denon AV receivers from roughly 2014 on, on the same Wi-Fi or LAN as your Android device. No cloud account or extra hardware. See the supported receivers.
Does it work on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows too?
Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad from the App Store, on Mac from the Mac App Store and on Windows from the Microsoft Store.
Is it free? Any ads or subscriptions?
It is a one-time purchase with no ads, no subscriptions and no account, and it controls your receiver directly over your local network. Home Automation (Home Assistant, Hubitat, webhooks) is an optional one-time unlock inside the app.
