Settings

Settings, section by section

Open Settings from the bottom tab bar. The page starts with your receivers, then the app's look, its layout, the add-ons, help, and backup. Settings for one receiver, such as its volume step or its locks, live on that receiver's own page.

Settings › Look & Feel

Make it look the way you like

  • Theme: Light, Dark or AMOLED (true black).
  • Accent color: Brand keeps the signature orange. Color Theme opens a picker: choose any color, or type a hex code such as #FF8C00 (or the three-character shorthand), and the app rebuilds its palette around it. A code the app cannot read is refused with the reason, and a color that is almost black is refused because it would have nothing to show against on the AMOLED theme. The round swatch next to the switch reopens the picker.
  • Display Size: a slider from Small through Default to XL that scales the app's text.
  • Widget Size: Small, Medium, Large or Larger. It scales the cards, buttons and spacing inside the app.
  • High-contrast widgets and Widgets follow app colour (phones and tablets): how your home-screen widgets are colored. High contrast uses greyscale tones; otherwise the widgets use the signature orange, or your Color Theme accent when Widgets follow app colour is on. Widgets guide →
  • Language: System default follows your device; or pick any app language by its own name. Languages guide →
Settings › Layout

Choose your tabs

  • Customize Navigation: choose which tabs sit in the bottom bar and drag them into your order. A preview shows the result; tap Save to keep it or Reset to go back to the default.
  • Customize Tabs: hide the sub-views you don't use inside a tab.

Settings › Help

Explanation cards, the welcome guide and this manual

  • Show explanation cards: many pages carry a short banner that explains them. Tap a banner to expand it and long-press it to dismiss it. Turn this switch off to hide every banner across the app.
  • Restore dismissed cards: lists each banner you long-pressed away. Tap Restore on one, or Restore all. When nothing is hidden it reads No dismissed cards.
  • Welcome & Setup Guide: replays the intro and network setup tips you saw on first launch. Getting started →
  • App Manual: opens this online user guide.

To find one setting quickly, use search instead of scrolling. Search guide →

Settings › Backup & About

Backup, rating and support

  • Backup & Restore: export every app setting to a file, or restore from a backup. Backup guide →
  • Rate AVR Maestro: opens the store listing so you can leave a rating.
  • About AVR Maestro: version information, support and licenses. This is where you find the Discord Community, the Facebook Support Group and All Relevant Logs, which gathers the logs we need when something misbehaves.
  • More from MiyaraHub: opens the MiyaraHub page on your device's store, with our other home-theater apps.
Common questions

Settings FAQ

Where did Settings › Devices and Settings › Audio go?
Since version 5.3.0 both live per receiver. Open Settings › Manage Receivers and tap your receiver: its page has the Audio section (volume step, volume readout, volume presets, device volume buttons and Volume Controls), Screen & Locks, About this receiver, and Rename, Change IP, Find My Receiver and Delete.
Can I change the app's theme and colors?
Yes. In Settings › Look & Feel, Theme is Light, Dark or AMOLED (true black), and Accent color is Brand or Color Theme. Color Theme lets you pick any accent from the picker or by typing a hex code such as #FF8C00 or its three-character shorthand. A code the app cannot read is refused with the reason, and a color that is almost black is refused because it would not show on the AMOLED theme.
Do my phone's volume buttons control the receiver?
Yes, on Android and on iPhone and iPad. While the app is open, the device's volume buttons change the receiver's volume. It is on by default and set per receiver: Settings › Manage Receivers › your receiver › Audio › Device volume buttons.
How do I bring back an explanation card I dismissed?
Open Settings › Help. Restore dismissed cards lists every card you long-pressed away; tap Restore on one, or Restore all. Show explanation cards turns all of them off or on at once.
How do I see the welcome guide again?
Open Settings › Help › Welcome & Setup Guide. It replays the intro and the network setup tips. App Manual in the same section opens this online guide.
Can I back up my setup?
Yes. Settings › Backup & Restore exports every app setting to a file and restores from a backup. See the Backup & Restore guide for what the file holds.
How do I open the hidden debug menu?
Open Settings › About AVR Maestro and tap the version number seven times. The debug menu then stays available until you remove it with Remove Debug Menu. It is meant for support; to send us logs, use All Relevant Logs on the same page.
