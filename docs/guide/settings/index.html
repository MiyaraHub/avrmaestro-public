Settings

Settings: make it yours

Everything that tailors AVR Maestro to you and your gear - manage receivers, theme the whole app with Color Theme, tune the volume step, grab a debug log for support, and back up your entire setup to a file.

Beyond the app

Home-screen widgets and device volume buttons

  • Home-screen widgets (iOS and Android): Quick Control (power / volume / mute / source), Now Playing, and Status - in multiple sizes, tap-only and auto-refreshing. When the receiver is off or unreachable, widgets show a clear "AVR off" / "Standby" state instead of stale input and volume - and the power button stays live, so you can wake the receiver straight from the widget.
  • Device volume buttons (Android and iOS): while AVR Maestro is in the foreground, the device's physical volume buttons control the receiver's volume (using your Volume Step), and holding one ramps. Foreground-only; the buttons go back to the device the moment the app is behind another, and the switch under Settings, Audio turns the takeover off.
Widgets, theming, Backup & Restore and the volume-button takeover all work on both iOS and Android.
Common questions

Settings FAQ

Can I change the app's theme and colors?
Yes - Light, Dark, Device or AMOLED (true black), plus Color Theme where you pick any accent color and the app rebuilds its palette. Use the wheel or type a hex code (#FF8C00, or the three-character shorthand). If the code is wrong the app tells you what is wrong with it rather than just refusing. You can also set Display/Widget size and customize navigation and tabs.
Can I back up my setup?
Yes - Settings, Backup exports every receiver, preset and preference to a .zip file that also bundles your custom input icons; import to restore on a new phone. Older .json backups still import fine. No credentials in the file, so it's safe to share.
Do volume presets jump straight to the target, or ramp there?
Your call - Settings, Audio has a Gradual / Direct switch. Gradual (the default) ramps smoothly toward a preset's target so a tap can never slam your speakers with a sudden jump. Direct applies it instantly. Works on the main zone and Zone 2/3.
Can I lock the receiver's physical remote from the app?
Yes, on receivers that support it - Settings, Receiver has a Remote Lock switch to lock or unlock the receiver's own IR remote.
Are the home-screen widgets Android-only?
No - Quick Control (power / volume / mute / source), Now Playing and Status widgets ship on both iOS and Android. When the receiver is off or unreachable they show a clear "AVR off" / "Standby" state instead of stale data, and the Quick Control power button stays live so you can wake the receiver from the widget.
Do the phone's volume keys control the receiver?
On Android, yes - while the app is in the foreground, your volume keys control the receiver's volume. iOS doesn't allow this.
How do I open the hidden debug menu?
Open Settings, About, and tap the version number seven times. The debug menu then stays available across launches - a live debug log plus raw command tools and diagnostics for support - until you tap "Remove Debug Menu" to hide it again.
What is "More from MiyaraHub"?
A section in Settings with a Browse all our apps button. Tap it and your app store opens to the MiyaraHub developer page - Google Play, the App Store or the Microsoft Store, depending on your device - so you can browse every app we make.
