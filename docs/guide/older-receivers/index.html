AVR-X4000 generation

Older Denon receivers, fully supported

AVR Maestro controls the AVR-X4000 generation of Denon receivers over your network. An older receiver answers fewer questions than a current one, so AVR Maestro shows a control only when your receiver reports it, and nothing is made up.

What you get

Controls on an older receiver

  • Favorites 1 to 4: the System page has a Favorites row for the main zone. Tap a slot to recall it; hold it to save what is playing, after you confirm.
  • Video Select: choose the video source from the System page.
  • Aspect Ratio and Video Resolution: set them from the System page, with the HDMI resolution beside them.
  • Network Presets: a panel on the Remote page. Tap a preset to recall it; hold it to store the current station, after you confirm.
  • HD Radio: on North American models, an HD Radio panel on the Radio page, once the receiver reports an HD station.
  • Zone Favorites and Sleep Timer: each zone card has four Favorites, recalled with a tap and saved with a hold, and a Sleep Timer of Off, 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes.
Every row above appears only when the receiver answers it. If a row is missing, your receiver did not report it, and that is the receiver, not the app.
What is not offered

Controls an older receiver cannot answer

Bluetooth, Loudness and Cinema EQ are not offered on this generation, because the receiver cannot answer them. AVR Maestro leaves them out instead of showing a switch that does nothing.

Common questions

Older receivers FAQ

Does AVR Maestro work with an older Denon such as the AVR-X4000?
Yes. It controls the main zone, Zone 2 and Zone 3 over the network. Older receivers answer fewer questions than current ones, so a control appears only when your receiver answers it.
Why are some controls missing on my older receiver?
The app shows a control only when the receiver reports it. An older Denon has no Bluetooth, Loudness or Cinema EQ to answer for, so those are not offered.
How do I save a Favorite on an older receiver?
Tap a Favorite to recall it. Hold it to save what is playing there, and confirm when asked.
