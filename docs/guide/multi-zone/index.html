Multi-zone

Multi-zone: run the whole house

Independent power, input and volume for Zone 2 and Zone 3 on multi-zone Denon and Marantz receivers - and Scenes can capture and restore zone state too. Music in the kitchen, the movie in the theater, all from one app.

How zones work

Each zone, fully independent

Each zone gets its own power, volume, mute, input and quick-volume presets, completely separate from the main zone.

🔌

Independent power

Turn Zone 2 or Zone 3 on and off without touching the main zone.

🎵

Own source & volume

A different input and volume per zone, with quick-volume preset chips - presets ramp smoothly by default (Settings, Audio: Gradual / Direct).

⚡

Zone Smart Select / Quick Select

The zone's own Smart Select or Quick Select slots, right on the card - tap a slot to recall it, press and hold to save the zone's current state into it.

✍️

Rename any zone

Long-press the zone name on the Home screen - main zone included. The name saves to the receiver itself, so every app agrees on it.

🔄

Live sync

Change a zone from the receiver's panel or remote and the app updates within a second.

🎧

Per-zone now playing

When a zone is on, its card shows the live HEOS stream for that zone with transport.

🎭

Captured by Scenes

A Scene can capture and restore Zone 2/3 state along with everything else.

📱

On the Home screen

A compact Zones widget gives at-a-glance status and quick controls on the Home screen.

Turning a zone on, and pointing it somewhere

  • 1. Open the Zones tab. Every zone your receiver declares gets a card. A receiver with one zone does not get the tab.
  • 2. Turn the zone on with the power control on its card. Nothing about the main zone changes.
  • 3. Pick its source. The list is your receiver's own, under any names you have renamed, and a zone can sit on a completely different input to the main room.
  • 4. Set its volume. The zone has its own level and its own mute, and the quick-volume chips recall levels you set on that zone. Presets ramp smoothly unless you switch to Direct under Settings, Audio.
  • 5. Optional: save it. Press and hold one of the zone's own Smart Select or Quick Select slots to store everything the zone is doing right now, then tap it later to bring it back.

Rename a zone by long-pressing its name on the Home screen. The name is written to the receiver, so the front panel and every other app agree with the app.

Worth knowing

Why powering the main zone does not kill your other rooms

Denon and Marantz receivers have two different power commands, and the difference matters if music is playing somewhere else in the house.

The system-wide standby command switches the whole chassis off, and the receiver takes the other zones down with it - measured on a Cinema 30, the chassis reports Zone 2 off one millisecond after the standby command lands. So the main power control in AVR Maestro deliberately sends the main-zone command instead. Turn the theatre off from the app and the kitchen keeps playing.

It works the other way too: switching the main zone on from a cold receiver wakes the chassis first, so you do not need to power the receiver on separately before using the app.

If a zone does switch itself off when the main zone changes, that is the receiver's own setup doing it rather than the app. Some models have a power-interlock option in their zone menu.
Common questions

Multi-zone FAQ

Can I control Zone 2 and Zone 3 from my phone?
Yes - on multi-zone receivers each zone has its own power, volume, mute, input and quick-volume presets, fully separate from the main zone, and syncs live.
Can I use Smart Select or Quick Select in a zone?
Yes - each zone card carries the zone's own slots, named the way your receiver names them: Smart Select on a Marantz, Quick Select on a Denon. Tap a slot to recall it; press and hold to save the zone's current state into it, straight from the card.
Can I rename my zones?
Yes - long-press the zone name on the Home screen to rename any zone, the main zone included. The name is written to the receiver itself, so it shows in the manufacturer's app and every other client, older chassis included.
Does each zone show what's playing?
Yes - when a zone is on, its card shows the live HEOS now-playing stream for that zone with transport controls.
Can a Scene set up my zones?
Yes - a Scene can capture and restore Zone 2/3 power, input and volume along with the rest of your setup.
