Getting around

Tabs, swipes and gestures

How to move around AVR Maestro: the bottom tabs and how to change them, swiping between pages, what a tap and a long press do, the Back button, the update prompt, messages, and keyboard shortcuts on a computer.

The bottom tabs

The bar at the bottom of the screen holds your main tabs. Out of the box they are Home, Remote, Sound, Zones, Scenes, Speakers and Settings. Radio can be added too. A search button sits at the right end of the bar on every tab (see Search).

  • Home is always included and always first.
  • Radio appears only when the selected receiver has a tuner. See Radio.
  • You can keep between 2 and 7 tabs.
Step by step

Add, remove or reorder tabs

  1. Open Settings › Customize Navigation. A preview of the bar is shown at the top.
  2. Under Available Tabs, tick a tab to add it or untick it to remove it.
  3. Under Active Tabs, drag a row by its handle to change the order.
  4. Tap Save. The message Navigation updated confirms it. Reset goes back to the default set and order.
Step by step

Hide sub-tabs you do not use

The Sound, Speakers and Remote tabs each have a row of sub-tabs along the top, such as Audyssey, Dirac Live, Graphic EQ, Channels and Tone on Sound.

  1. Open Settings › Customize Tabs (Hide sub-views you don't use).
  2. Under Sound, Speakers or Remote, switch off each sub-view you do not want. The preview shows the result.
  3. Go back. The change applies to every receiver.
If your receiver does not support a feature, for example Audyssey on a receiver without Audyssey, its sub-tab is already hidden and is not listed here. Customize Tabs is a second layer on top of that.

Swipe between pages

  • Swipe left or right on a page to move to the next or previous sub-tab.
  • Keep swiping at the last sub-tab and you move on to the next bottom tab. Past the last tab it wraps round to the first, in the order you set.
  • Swipe along the bottom tab bar to switch tabs directly. This wraps round too.
  • On a page with sliders, a slow drag moves the slider. Changing page needs a quick, mostly sideways flick.

Swipe back a step on iPhone, iPad and Mac

In the guided Scene creator, the Welcome & Setup Guide and the Home Assistant sign-in page, swipe in from the left edge of the screen to go back one step. In right-to-left languages, swipe in from the right edge. Swipe more than halfway across, or flick quickly.

Tap and long press

Many controls do one thing on a tap and something else when you press and hold.

  • Volume presets. There are 8 preset slots. Tap an empty slot marked + to save the current volume to it. Tap a saved slot to jump to that volume. Long press any slot to save the current volume over it or clear it. To fade to a preset instead of jumping, turn on Gradual volume presets in Settings › Manage Receivers › your receiver › Audio. See Volume.
  • Quick Select and Smart Select. Tap a slot to recall it. Long press for Save current state or Rename.
  • Home input grid. Long press to start editing the grid. See Home Screen.
  • Remote inputs. Long press an input to give it a custom icon. See Custom Icons.
  • Zone names. Long press a zone's name to rename it. See Multi-Zone.
  • Radio presets. Tap to recall, long press to save the current station. See Radio.
  • Scenes. Tap to apply, hold for options. See Scenes.
  • Receivers. In Settings › Manage Receivers, long press a receiver for its options.
  • Explanation cards. Tap a banner to expand it, long press to dismiss it. Bring them back from Settings › Restore dismissed cards.

Back, exit and messages

  • Back on a sub-page returns you to the top of the tab. On Home while editing, Back leaves edit mode first.
  • Back at the top of a tab asks Exit AVR Maestro? Tap Exit App to close the app, or Cancel to stay. This applies to the Android Back button and Back gesture.
  • Messages at the bottom of the screen have a close button. Error messages stay until you close them. Other messages close on their own after a few seconds, longer for longer messages. A new message replaces the one on screen.
With your phone's accessible navigation setting on, every message stays until you close it.

When an update is available

When a newer version of AVR Maestro is published, the Home screen shows Update available.

  • Update opens the app's page in Google Play, the App Store or the Microsoft Store.
  • Later hides the prompt for 3 days.
  • Skip this version stops the prompt for that version.
If a version is marked as required, the prompt says Update required, has only the Update button and cannot be dismissed.

Keyboard shortcuts on Windows and Mac

Use Ctrl on Windows or Cmd on Mac with:

  • Up / Down arrow: volume up or down
  • M: mute on or off
  • Space: play or pause
  • Right / Left arrow: next or previous track
  • Period (.): stop

The media keys on a keyboard (play, pause, next, previous, stop, volume and mute) work too.

Shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a text field. Mute and play or pause wait until the receiver has reported its current state.

Platform notes

  • Android: Back asks before exiting. The phone's volume buttons can change the receiver's volume while the app is open (Device volume buttons, per receiver, on by default).
  • iPhone and iPad: swipe in from the edge to step back in guided flows. Device volume buttons works here too.
  • Mac: edge swipe to step back, Cmd shortcuts and media keys.
  • Windows: Ctrl shortcuts and media keys.
FAQ

Navigation, answered

How do I change which tabs are at the bottom of AVR Maestro?
Open Settings › Customize Navigation. Untick a tab under Available Tabs to remove it, tick one to add it, drag the rows under Active Tabs to change the order, then tap Save. Home is always included and always first. You can have 2 to 7 tabs.
How do I hide a sub-tab I never use, such as IMAX or Auro-3D?
Open Settings › Customize Tabs and switch off the sub-views you do not want under Sound, Speakers or Remote. The choice applies to every receiver. Features your receiver does not support are already hidden and are not listed there.
Why is there no Radio tab?
Radio can be added in Settings › Customize Navigation, but the tab only appears when the selected receiver has a tuner.
Can I swipe between tabs?
Yes. Swipe left or right on a page to move through its sub-tabs. At the last sub-tab the next swipe moves to the next bottom tab, and from the last tab it wraps round to the first. You can also swipe along the bottom tab bar itself.
Why does AVR Maestro ask before closing?
On Android, pressing Back at the top of a tab asks Exit AVR Maestro? so a stray Back press does not close the app. Tap Exit App to close it or Cancel to stay. From a sub-page, Back first returns you to the top of the tab.
Does AVR Maestro have keyboard shortcuts on Windows and Mac?
Yes. Ctrl on Windows or Cmd on Mac plus the Up or Down arrow changes the volume, plus M toggles mute, plus Space plays or pauses, plus the Left or Right arrow skips back or forward, and plus the period key stops. Media keys on the keyboard work too. Shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a text field.
