Manage Receivers, one page per receiver

Add as many Denon and Marantz receivers as you have, switch between them in two taps, and give each one its own audio preferences. Every receiver has its own page for its name, address, locks and firmware.

Where it lives

Open Manage Receivers

Open Settings. The card at the very top is Manage Receivers: it shows how many receivers you have and which one is active. Tap it.

What you will see: the line "Tap a receiver to set it up. Tap the circle to make it active. Drag to reorder.", then your receivers, each with its name, IP address and model. The active receiver is highlighted and its circle is filled. Below the list are the Add Receiver button and, unless you are already in it, a DEMO MODE section.

  • Tap a receiver to open its own page.
  • Tap the circle at the start of a row to make that receiver active. The app says Switched to and the receiver's name.
  • Drag the handle at the end of a row to change the order. The receiver list at the top of Home follows the same order.
  • Long-press a receiver for a quick menu with Rename, Change IP, Find My Receiver and Delete.
Step by step

Add a receiver

  1. Open Settings › Manage Receivers and tap Add Receiver (or the + at the top right).
  2. On iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows the screen scans your network and lists Receivers found on your network. Tap yours. If nothing answers, tap Scan again or type the receiver's address in IP Address and tap Test & Save.
  3. The app tests the connection and saves the receiver in one go. You will see the detected model and features, and the new receiver becomes the active one.
The receiver's IP address is in its own menu under Setup › General › Network › Information. The phone or computer must be on the same network as the receiver. See Getting started for the network checklist.
Two taps

Switch receivers from Home

  1. On the Home tab, tap the receiver name shown next to AVR Maestro at the top of the screen.
  2. A list of your receivers opens, with a check next to the active one. Tap another receiver to switch to it, or tap Manage Receivers at the bottom of the list to open the full page.

Each receiver keeps its own data on your device, so switching loads that receiver's settings straight away.

The receiver page

Tap a receiver to open its page. The card at the top shows its connection state and details. If it is not the active receiver, a Make active button sits under the card, because the volume limits, display and locks can only be changed on the active receiver. If it is active but not reachable, the page says "The app is not connected to the receiver right now."

AUDIO

Your own preferences for this receiver. Each receiver keeps its own values. Volume guide →

  • Volume Step: how far one press moves the volume, 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2 dB. Every volume button in the app uses it.
  • Show absolute volume on top: swaps which number is big in the volume readout. Off (the default) shows dB big and the volume scale small; on shows the volume scale big and dB small.
  • Remote volume display: whether the Remote tab shows the dB scale (Absolute (dB), for example -40.5 dB) or the 0-98 scale (Relative, for example 39.5).
  • Gradual volume presets: on (the default), tapping a volume preset ramps the volume in small steps; off, it jumps straight to the saved level.
  • Device volume buttons (phones and tablets): while the app is open, the device's own volume buttons change the receiver's volume. On by default; turn it off to give the buttons back to the device.
  • Volume Controls: volume limit, power-on level and mute depth, written to the receiver itself. Shown while the app is connected to this receiver. Volume Controls guide →

SCREEN & LOCKS

Shown for the active receiver while connected.

  • On-Screen Display: volume readout, info banner, now playing and 21:9. On-Screen Display guide →
  • Setup Lock: locks the receiver's own setup menu. While it is locked, the Audio, Video, Speaker and other setup settings cannot be changed anywhere, not in the receiver's menu, not in this app and not in its web portal. Everyday controls such as volume, input and dimmer keep working. Turning it on asks first (Lock the receiver menu?, then Lock); turn the switch off to unlock.
  • Remote Lock: makes the receiver ignore button presses from its IR remote until you unlock it. The app and the front panel keep working.
Setup Lock and Remote Lock appear only when the receiver reports their state. If your receiver does not report a lock, the switch is not shown rather than guessed. A control that is greyed out elsewhere in the app because of Setup Lock is explained in Why a control is locked.

ABOUT THIS RECEIVER

  • Device Information: the receiver's detailed status.
  • Firmware: the installed firmware version. Tap Check to ask the receiver whether an update is available. The line underneath then says you are on the latest firmware, that an update is available, that an update is in progress, or that the status could not be read. An update is installed from the receiver's own Setup menu, not from the app; do not power the receiver off during an update.

MANAGE

  • Rename: type a new name in Device Name and tap Save. The name is only stored in the app.
  • Change IP: type a new IP Address or Hostname and tap Save, for example after your router handed out a new address. All saved settings for the receiver are kept; only the address changes. An address already used by another of your receivers is refused.
  • Find My Receiver: searches the network for this receiver's new address. If it finds your receiver somewhere else, tap Use This Address. It tells you when the saved address is already correct, when nothing answered, and when more than one receiver could be yours, in which case it changes nothing and you use Change IP instead.
  • Delete: asks Delete Receiver? and then removes the receiver and all its saved settings from the app. Deleting the active receiver switches the app to another one.
No receiver needed

Demo Mode, in and out

Demo Mode runs a simulated Marantz CINEMA 30 inside the app. Every screen works as it does with real hardware, and nothing is sent over your network.

  1. Start it from Settings › Manage Receivers › Try Demo Mode, from Explore Demo Mode on the Add Receiver screen, or from Try the demo instead in the welcome guide. The demo receiver becomes active and the app opens Home.
  2. Preview Pro. While the demo runs, Settings shows a DEMO MODE section. Its Preview Pro features switch unlocks Home Automation and Triggers for this demo session only. Nothing is purchased.
  3. Leave it with Settings › Leave Demo Mode. The demo receiver is removed and the app goes back to your own receiver.
Common questions

Receivers, answered

How do I switch to another receiver?
On the Home tab, tap the receiver name next to AVR Maestro at the top and pick another receiver from the list. You can also open Settings › Manage Receivers and tap the circle at the start of a receiver's row. The app confirms with Switched to and the receiver's name.
My receiver got a new IP address. What do I do?
Usually nothing: when the router moves a receiver, the app finds it again and tells you once that it followed it. If it cannot, open Settings › Manage Receivers, tap the receiver and use Find My Receiver to search the network, or Change IP to type the new address yourself. Every saved setting for the receiver is kept; only the address changes.
Why do I not see Screen & Locks or About this receiver?
Those sections read and write the receiver live, so they appear only for the active receiver while the app is connected to it. Make the receiver active with Make active, and wait for it to connect.
What is the difference between Setup Lock and Remote Lock?
Setup Lock locks the receiver's own setup menu, so its setup and calibration settings cannot be changed by anyone, including this app, until you unlock it. Volume and input keep working. Remote Lock makes the receiver ignore its IR remote control; the app and the front panel keep working. Each switch appears only when your receiver reports that lock.
What happens when I delete a receiver?
The app removes the receiver and all saved settings for it. If it was the active receiver, the app switches to another one. Nothing is changed on the receiver itself, and you can add it again any time.
Can I try the app without a receiver?
Yes. Tap Try Demo Mode in Settings › Manage Receivers, or Explore Demo Mode on the Add Receiver screen. Demo Mode runs a simulated Marantz CINEMA 30 inside the app and sends nothing over your network. Leave it from Settings › Leave Demo Mode.
