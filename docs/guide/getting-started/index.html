Getting started

Control your Denon or Marantz from your phone

AVR Maestro talks to your receiver over your local network using its native protocol - no cloud account, no extra hardware. A short welcome guide gets you connected and building your first Scene in a couple of minutes.

Your first launch

A five-step welcome that sets you up

The first time you open AVR Maestro, a short guide walks you from zero to your first Scene. A bar at the top shows how far along you are, Back takes you to any earlier step, and Skip is always there if you would rather explore on your own.

  1. Welcome. What AVR Maestro is: full control of your Denon or Marantz over your own network, with nothing going through the cloud. It is just as clear about what it is not: an independent app, not a calibration tool and not a streaming app, so you know what to expect before you start.
  1. Your receivers. The app scans your network and lists every receiver it finds. Tap yours to add it, and add more than one if you have them. Nothing listed? Use Add by IP address, or tap Try the demo instead to look around with a demo receiver that comes with Scenes ready to try.
  1. Where things live. A quick map of the app: Home for power, volume and input in every zone, Sound for sound modes, Audyssey and Dirac, and Scenes for your saved setups.
  1. Scenes. What a Scene does in one tap, and how it goes further than your receiver's own Smart Select or Quick Select slots: up to 20 Scenes, home screen widgets and shortcuts, and a check afterwards that the receiver really took every setting. Tap Build my first scene and the guided Scene builder opens right there.
  1. You're set. Where to get help: the Discord community and the Facebook support group, and how to share your logs from Settings → About → All Relevant Logs if something is not right.
Want the tour again, or set the app up on a new receiver? Open Settings → Welcome & Setup Guide any time. See Settings.
Before you start

What you need

  • An iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android phone or tablet, or Windows PC on the same network as the receiver.
  • The receiver powered on with Network Standby enabled (Setup → General → Network → Network Control → On) so the app can wake and reach it.
  • Nothing else: the app finds the receiver on the network by itself. If your network keeps devices from seeing each other, the receiver's IP address (Setup → General → Network → Information) does instead.
  • Optional: enable Wake-on-LAN to power the receiver from cold standby.
Adding a receiver later

More than one receiver?

  1. Open Settings → Devices and tap Add Receiver.
  2. The screen scans your network and lists every Denon or Marantz receiver it hears. Tap yours (or type its IP address if nothing answers) and the app probes HTTP, Telnet and HEOS.
  3. Confirm the detected model and capabilities to save it.
  4. Switch between receivers any time from Settings → Devices. The app remembers the receiver itself, not its address: when your router moves it, the app follows and tells you once.
Troubleshooting

If it won't connect

"Test Connection" fails
Make sure the phone and receiver are on the same Wi-Fi network and subnet - mesh systems, guest networks and IoT VLANs can isolate devices. On the receiver, Setup → Network → Network Control must be On. Re-check the IP against the receiver's own Network → Information screen, since DHCP addresses change. HTTP is required; Telnet and HEOS are nice-to-have. If the receiver answers but slowly, the app tells you its network is likely busy rather than claiming the address is wrong - confirm the receiver loads in a browser or the official app, then power-cycle it to clear its network stack.
Connection drops every few seconds
Receiver Telnet ports can get sticky after long uptimes - power-cycle the receiver from the wall to clear the listener. On Wi-Fi 6 mesh, force the phone onto the same band as the receiver (AVRs usually sit on 2.4 GHz; phones prefer 5 GHz).
How do I know my receiver is supported?
It comes down to your network, not just the model. If the receiver's web portal, the HEOS app and the official Denon/Marantz app can all reach it, AVR Maestro should too. See the supported receivers page for the model list.
