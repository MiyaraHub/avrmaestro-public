Stop scrolling through menus

Search, from any tab

A receiver hides hundreds of settings behind tabs, sub-tabs and menus. Type a few letters of the one you want, tap the result, and AVR Maestro opens the right page, scrolls to the setting and flashes it so you can see where it is.

Two ways in

  • The Home search box. It sits on the Home tab under the receiver card. Tap it and start typing.
  • The search button. At the right end of the bottom tab bar, on every tab. It opens the same search in a sheet over the page you are on, so you never have to go back to Home first.
Step by step

Find a setting in three taps

  1. Type a word. Part of a name is enough: dialog finds Dialog Enhancer and Dialog & DRC. A single wrong letter is forgiven, so volune still finds Volume Limit.
  2. Pick a result. The first 8 are shown. If there are more, a Show all row lists the rest. Results starting with what you typed come first, then names containing it, then related keywords.
  3. Land on the setting. The app opens the right tab and sub-tab, scrolls to the setting and pulses it for a moment. Change it right there.
What it covers

Receiver settings and the app itself

  • Sound: Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume, Dialog Enhancer, Center Level, Tone Control, Audyssey MultEQ, the Dirac Live filter and more.
  • System: HDMI output, triggers, Eco, auto standby, sleep timer, HDMI CEC, picture and video modes, audio delay, dimmer and more.
  • Receiver pages: Manage Receivers, Volume Controls, On-Screen Display and Device Information.
  • The app: language, navigation and tab views, the assistant, backup, widget size, high-contrast widgets and the About page. These carry the label App and work even with no receiver selected.
  • Actions: rename a receiver, change its IP address, find my receiver, delete it, rate the app, share logs, open the manual, replay the welcome guide, and try or leave Demo Mode.
Honest results

It only offers what your receiver has

Search asks the connected receiver what it can do, so a setting your chassis does not have is never listed. Switch to another receiver and the results change with it, without retyping.

A setting the receiver has but cannot change right now is shown dimmed with the reason underneath, for example that the current sound mode locks it. A setting the receiver has not reported yet reads Unknown. The app never turns "not answered yet" into "not available".

Every language

Type it your way

Search works in all of the app's languages. Accents, umlauts, the Turkish dotted and dotless i, full-width letters, Arabic vowel marks and Hebrew points do not have to be typed. The English name of a setting also finds it in any language, listed after the matches in your own language, because the receiver's own menus and manual use English terms.

Common questions

Search, answered

Where is the search in AVR Maestro?
On the Home tab there is a search box under the receiver card. On every tab there is also a search button at the right end of the bottom tab bar. Both open the same search.
What can I search for?
Receiver settings on the Sound, Speakers, Zones and System pages, the app's own options such as language, backup and widget size, and a few actions such as renaming a receiver or leaving Demo Mode. Results from the app itself carry the label App.
Why is a result greyed out?
A grey result is a setting your receiver has but cannot change right now. The line under it says why, for example that the current sound mode locks it. A result marked Unknown means the receiver has not answered yet, and the app does not guess.
Why is a setting I am looking for not listed?
Because your receiver does not have it. AVR Maestro lists only what the connected receiver offers, so an older receiver simply never shows settings it lacks. If you have two receivers, the results follow whichever one is selected.
Does search work in my language?
Yes. Search works in every app language. You do not need to type accents, and the English name of a setting finds it in any language, listed after the matches in your own language.
