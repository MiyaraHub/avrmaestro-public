Is there a better app than the official Denon or Marantz app?
Many owners use AVR Maestro instead. It connects over your network using the receiver's native protocols, responds instantly, and surfaces controls the official apps bury or omit - one-tap Scenes, full Audyssey and Dirac Live, multi-zone, speaker presets and an FM/AM tuner.
Does AVR Maestro work with my receiver?
It works with network-enabled Denon and Marantz AV receivers, including the Marantz Cinema 30/40/50/60 series and Denon AVR-A1H, X3800H, X4800H, X6800H and AVR-S770H/S970H. No cloud account or extra hardware - just the same Wi-Fi or LAN as the receiver.
Is it free? Any ads or subscriptions?
It is a one-time purchase with no ads, no subscriptions and no account, and it controls your receiver directly over your local network. Available on Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store.
Can it control Audyssey and Dirac Live?
Yes. On receivers that expose them, AVR Maestro reads and switches Audyssey MultEQ curves and Dirac Live slots directly, alongside surround modes, channel levels, speaker presets and multi-zone control.
